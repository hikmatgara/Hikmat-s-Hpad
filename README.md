# Hikmat's Hpad
Im building my own HackPad for my stardance project!

I want to build a 3 key hackpad for shortcuts I went with a minimalist design,
first I designed the PCB and then the schematic.
Later I started designing the case and the panel of the hackpad I spent hours in fusion360 and couldn't even put holes in the case that is why I decided to use some help of AI to help me design the case.
Overall I learnt a lot from this guide especially what I loved the most is how to design the PCB's its very interesting and will be useful in the future.
